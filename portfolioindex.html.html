Learning security by building it, not just reading about it.

I'm a B.Tech Cybersecurity student, self-studying networking and security from the ground up through a structured curriculum I built for myself — covering everything from OSI-layer fundamentals to vulnerability assessment, with hands-on labs on TryHackMe and PortSwigger along the way.
